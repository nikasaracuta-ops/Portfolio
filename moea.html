The Brief

A client has heard that 3D is the next big thing on the web and wants it on their website as a promotional header.

Imagining I worked at a design agency, I could pick the brand myself, so I chose a responsible one: Moea, short for Mother Earth, a label that rethinks how fashion is made, with respect for the planet.
The hero had to tell a visual concept, a little world built around the product, and couldn’t be static: animation brings it to life. I designed and animated it in Blender.

Visual Style

Before modelling in Blender, a mood and styleboard showed the client where the little world around the product was heading.
