Imagine working at a design agency for brands big and small: a client has heard that 3D is the next big thing on the web and wants it on their website as a promotional header. I could pick the brand myself, so I chose a responsible one: Moea, short for Mother Earth, a label that rethinks how fashion is made, with respect for the planet.

The hero had to tell a visual concept, a little world built around the product, didn’t need to be photorealistic, and couldn’t be static: animation brings it to life. I designed and animated it in Blender, starting from a mood and styleboard so the client could see where it was heading.
