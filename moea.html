The Brief

A client has heard that 3D is the next big thing on the web and wants it on their website as a promotional header.

For this assignment, I could pick the brand myself, so I chose Moea, short for Mother Earth, a label that rethinks how fashion is made, with respect for the planet.
The hero had to tell a visual concept, a little world built around the product, and needed to be animated (the animation is coming soon!).

Visual Style

I wanted to go for a chrome style in combination with natural elements: liquid metal next to stone, moss, flowers and water.
