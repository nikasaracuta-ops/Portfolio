01

The Brief

Miles & Meals is a food-box subscription that brings iconic local dishes from cities around the world to your kitchen. Every box explores a different city through authentic recipes, inspired by how locals really eat. The founders want to launch in Antwerp in summer 2026, and grow to many more cities after that.

As lead designer, I worked in two parts. First the visual identity: a brand that speaks to its audience and values, presented in a brandboard with its personality, tone of voice, logo, colours, typography, photography, icons, packaging and social media.

Then the app, where users manage their box and cook the recipes. I started from the existing wireframes but approached them critically, looking for smarter solutions, clear hierarchy, helpful feedback and small interactions that make you smile, all finished in a clickable prototype.
