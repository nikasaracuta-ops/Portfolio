The Brief

Miles & Meals is a food-box subscription that brings iconic local dishes from cities around the world to your kitchen.

Every box explores a different city through authentic recipes, inspired by how locals really eat. The audience: adults aged 25–50 with middle to upper-middle incomes, and four brand values to build on: travel, warm & welcoming, out & social and authentic.
First I had to create the visual identity, presented in a brandboard, then design the app where users could manage their box and cook the recipes.

Visual Style

At first the style focused on linocut imagery and traditional patterns from different cultures, but during the app design process it became simpler.
