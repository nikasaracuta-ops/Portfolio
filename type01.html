TYPE01 Magazine, a publication for graphic designers and typographers, was hosting its first-ever conference: two days in London on typography in print, motion design and branding.

My task was to design a responsive conference website for desktop and mobile that fits TYPE01’s identity but takes a bolder approach.

Text and photos of the speakers were already provided.
