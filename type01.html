The Brief

TYPE01 Magazine, a publication for graphic designers and typographers, was hosting its first-ever conference: two days in London on typography in print, motion design and branding.

My task was to design a responsive conference website for desktop and mobile that fits TYPE01’s identity but takes a bolder approach. Text and photos of the speakers were already provided.

02

Visual process

1. Styleboard

I explored two identities, one for a light mode and one for a dark mode. Both mix bold grotesk display type with pixel and halftone patterns, collaged photography and a pink-and-green palette taken from TYPE01’s identity. The final website uses the light mode, and a switch to the dark mode could be added later as a feature.

2. Iterations

From the first wireframes to the light, dark and mobile versions of each page.
