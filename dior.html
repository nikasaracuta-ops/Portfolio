Case study

Dior

MoMu – Dress to Impress: an interactive longread for MoMu – Fashion Museum Antwerp

Client
MoMu – Fashion Museum Antwerp
Disciplines
  • UI
  • UX
  • Web design
  • Web development
Built with
Vite · GSAP · ScrollTrigger
01

The Fashion Museum of Antwerp, MoMu, wanted to launch its new digital marketing plan with a series of six interactive online stories, one published every two months, each about a fashion designer. My task was to design and build the first one: a one-page interactive longread about Walter Van Beirendonk, Christian Dior or Dirk Bikkembergs, told from my own angle.

Goal: turn a static designer story into an immersive, interactive experience that teaches visitors about fashion and makes them want to visit the museum, reaching people who hadn’t yet considered MoMu, mainly adults (with or without children). The design had to respect MoMu’s logo and tone of voice while giving the story its own visual identity.

Scope: research, visual design and front-end development. The site is responsive and mobile-first, built with Vite, GSAP and ScrollTrigger, and deployed online.

02

Archive photography of Dior’s couture, sketches and a soft, powdery palette of cream, rose and dusty blue set the tone of the story.

From photo research and the first desktop layouts to the scroll-driven chapters of the final story.
