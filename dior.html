The Brief

MoMu, the Fashion Museum of Antwerp, wants to launch its new digital marketing plan with a series of six interactive online stories, each about a fashion designer.

My task was to research, design and build the first one: a one-page interactive longread about Walter Van Beirendonk, Christian Dior or Dirk Bikkembergs, told from my own angle.
I chose Christian Dior and decided to write about the art of his dressmaking, turning his story into an interactive experience, from the first sketch to the finished gown.

Visual Style

Archive photography of Dior’s couture, fashion sketches and a soft, powdery palette. While designing, the colours changed towards cooler tones.
