01

MoMu, the Fashion Museum of Antwerp, launched its new digital marketing plan with a series of six interactive online stories, one every two months, each about a fashion designer.

My task was to design and build the first one: a one-page interactive longread about Walter Van Beirendonk, Christian Dior or Dirk Bikkembergs, told from my own angle. I chose Christian Dior.

Goal

Turn a static designer story into an immersive, interactive experience that teaches visitors about fashion and makes them want to visit the museum.

Audience

People who hadn’t yet considered MoMu, mainly adults, with or without children. The design respects MoMu’s logo and tone of voice, while giving the story its own visual identity.

Scope

Research, visual design and front-end development: a responsive, mobile-first site built with Vite, GSAP and ScrollTrigger, and deployed online.

02

Archive photos of Dior’s couture, fashion sketches and a soft, powdery palette of cream, rose and dusty blue set the tone for the story.

From the first photo research and desktop layouts to the scroll-driven chapters of the final story.
